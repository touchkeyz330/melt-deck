Put your own single-cycle waves here (.wav, 16 to 4096 points, one cycle each), or 2048-point
wavetables (each 2048-point frame becomes its own wave, up to 16 per file). They appear after
the 32 built-in waves in every Wave menu. Rebuild the plugin after adding waves so the screen
menus show their names. Only share waves you have the right to share.
